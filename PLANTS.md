# Plant list

A running checklist of every plant in the Garden Designer library, and every
plant we have agreed to add.

**Ticked** means the plant is in the app now. **Unticked** means it is planned
but not built — nothing gets added until Mela says so, batch by batch.

Each plant already in the app is marked one of two ways:

- **FULL** — everything the plant does in a real garden is modelled: its size and
  growth, its seasons, its flowers and fruit, its winter structure, and whether
  it is a plant that gets clipped.
- **PARTIAL** — the plant works and is safe to design with, but one thing it does
  in life is missing from the model. The line underneath says what.

Sourcing rule for every entry, existing and planned: facts are read from the
plant's RHS page and recorded as data; the description is written fresh for this
app; the RHS page is linked from the plant's own panel. No RHS text or
photographs are copied, and nothing is harvested automatically. See
`PRODUCT.md` for the model's stated limitations.

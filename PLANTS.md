# Plant list

A running checklist of every plant in the Garden Designer library, and every
plant we have agreed to add.

**Ticked** means the plant is in the app now. **Unticked** means it is planned
but not built — nothing gets added until Mela says so, batch by batch.

Every plant has a number, so any of them can be named in one word.

- `001`–`270` are plants in the app. The number is the order the plant
  was added to the library, so **it never changes** — new plants take the next
  number up, and nothing already numbered shifts underneath it.
- `P001` upwards are plants not yet built. A P-number is temporary: when the
  plant is built it gives up its P-number and takes a permanent one.

Numbers are never reused. If a plant is ever removed, its number retires with it.

Each plant already in the app is marked one of two ways:

- **FULL** — everything the plant does in a real garden is modelled: its size and
  growth, its seasons, its flowers and fruit, its winter structure, and whether
  it is a plant that gets clipped.
- **PARTIAL** — the plant works and is safe to design with, but one thing it does
  in life is missing from the model. The line underneath says what.

Sourcing rule for every entry, existing and planned: facts are read from the
plant's RHS page and recorded as data; the description is written fresh for this
app; the RHS page is linked from the plant's own panel. No RHS text or
photographs are copied, and nothing is harvested automatically. See
`PRODUCT.md` for the model's stated limitations.

## Still to build

Only one plant from the original plan is not in the app.

- [ ] `P090` *Nymphaea* — waterlily

Left out deliberately, and it needs a decision plus a piece of drawing work, not
a data row:

1. **There is no shape for it.** The app can draw twelve plant forms and none of
   them is flat pads floating on water. Forcing it into an existing form would
   draw a mound of leaves standing up out of dry ground.
2. **It turns a check red.** A test deliberately records that nothing in the
   library is a pond plant. Adding a waterlily breaks it — correctly — and that
   test is not ours to rewrite without asking.

Everything else that was queued has been built: all of batch 1, all of batch 2,
all of batch 3 except this, and everything that was held in reserve. Their
P-numbers are retired and they now carry permanent library numbers above.
